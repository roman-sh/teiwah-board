# Refund Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

We want you to be happy with Teiwah. This Refund Policy explains when you can get a refund for your subscription.

## 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the Service, you may request a full refund within **14 days** of your initial purchase. Just email us at **support@teiwah.cloud** from the address on your account and we will process the refund.

## Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month. Renewal charges are generally non-refundable. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel before your renewal date — you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing period.

We understand mistakes happen. If you were charged for a renewal you intended to cancel, contact us within **7 days** of the charge and we will review your request in good faith.

## How refunds are issued

Payments are processed by our reseller and Merchant of Record, **Lemon Squeezy**. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method by Lemon Squeezy. It may take a few business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.

## Service availability

Because the Service connects to WhatsApp, which we do not control, we cannot guarantee that a number will remain connected or that every message will be delivered. Temporary WhatsApp-side limits or bans on a number are not, on their own, grounds for a refund beyond the guarantees described above.

## How to request a refund

Email **support@teiwah.cloud** with the email address associated with your account and your order details. We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days.
